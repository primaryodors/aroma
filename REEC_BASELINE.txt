REEC baseline measurements
Date: 2026-11-08
Status: item 1 in progress

Harness

hm/reec_baseline.py prepares isolated experimental-template inputs and runs
matched native-engine comparisons with annealing disabled and with 64 rounds.
It does not publish receptor models or alter canonical active PDB files.

Initial matrix:
- OR1A1, OR1B1, OR51E1, OR51E2.
- Seeds 0 and 1, three candidates per batch, 3000 iterations per candidate.
- Annealing temperature 12, clash scale 1, BW binding-site residue targeting.
- Six candidates per receptor and annealing setting, 48 candidates overall.

Command from project root:
python3 hm/reec_baseline.py --output tmp/reec-baseline-2026-11-08 --models 3 --seeds 0 1 --iterations 3000

Use a fresh output directory for each repetition. The harness refuses to
overwrite an existing run directory. Build output, engine logs, exact commands,
input hashes, per-candidate reports, independently reviewed accepted models,
timings, and superposed pocket comparisons are retained there. summary.json
is updated after every completed batch.

Interpretation

These are native-engine controls, not full workflow or accuracy benchmarks.
They omit PHEW/internal-coordinate publication, retry escalation, workflow
contacts, and docking environments. OR1B1 generation is not the historical
maltol repair replay. OR51E2 generation covers the full sequence and does not
test publication of its intentionally truncated validated fragment.

The initial matrix uses experimental templates only. OR51E1 includes the
active experimental OR templates, without the absent validated OR51E2 PDB.
Only template entries present in experimental.ali are selected; for OR1A1
and OR1B1 this selects 8uxy and 9w45, rather than reconstructing historical custom
templates. Inputs are fixed across each annealing comparison.

Pocket diversity is common heavy-atom RMSD after superposition using common
CA atoms. Atom names establish correspondence; equivalent symmetric atoms
are not remapped. This is a diversity measurement, not experimental accuracy
or a conformer-clustering rule.

Timings are elapsed batch time on this machine. Disabled annealing precedes
enabled annealing within each seed. Treat timing ratios as exploratory;
repeated, counterbalanced runs would provide stronger performance estimates.

Metric checks

test/reec_baseline_test.py checks removal of rigid motion, detection of local
side-chain motion, explicit handling of insufficient correspondence, and
inclusion of rejected candidates in acceptance denominators.

Expanded annealing comparison

REEC_ANNEALING_COMPARISON.txt records a completed counterbalanced four-seed
comparison across OR1B1, OR51E1, OR1G1, OR2AT4, OR2T11, OR2AG1, OR5V1,
OR6P1, OR52E8, and TAAR5. All 240 candidate evaluations passed native and
independent geometry review. Annealing improved the pooled best objective
for OR2AG1 and OR5V1, worsened it for OR2AT4 and TAAR5, and left six unchanged.
Total engine time increased by 53.7 percent. These are optimization results;
structural accuracy and held-out docking comparisons remain separate work.
Seven harness checks pass. No production defaults were changed.
